Wanda Customer App Support

Official support for the Wanda customer app (com.mahazchowdhury.wanda).

Contact support
wandasoftwareqatar@gmail.com

We aim to acknowledge requests within 24–48 hours. Never email your passcode, authentication token or payment-card details.

What we can help with

  • Invitations, account registration, passcode login and access problems
  • Appointment availability, booking, cancellation or rescheduling
  • Profile, notification, package-wallet or salon-history questions
  • Incorrect information, privacy requests, account deletion and security concerns
  • Bug reports and accessibility feedback

Before contacting us

  • Confirm that the app is updated and your device has an internet connection.
  • For a missing invitation or incorrect salon record, contact the salon that registered you.
  • Include your name, registered phone number, device type and a description of the issue. Do not include your passcode.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the Customer Privacy Notice. You can delete your app account from Profile → Delete account, or use the external deletion-request page if you cannot access the app.
